Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact support about my complaint?

Email our complaints team at complaints-support@example-lloydsfinancial.co.uk or call our customer line. For Premier customers, your Relationship Manager, Priya Nair, can be reached directly on 0161 496 0147.

What is the overdraft interest rate?

The current arranged overdraft Effective Annual Rate (EAR) on a Premier Current Account is 19.9%, reviewed quarterly.

How long does a complaint take to resolve?

Most complaints are resolved within 10 working days of acknowledgement. You will receive a written update if it takes longer.

Can I get a mortgage with a 10% deposit?

Yes - our Standard and Premier mortgage products both lend up to 90% LTV, subject to the stressed-rate affordability check described in the Mortgage Lending SOP.

What savings rate do I get with a Premier account?

Premier Easy Saver pays 4.10% AER variable with instant access; the Premier Fixed Saver pays 4.55% AER fixed for one year.
